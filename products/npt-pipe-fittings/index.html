Custom NPT Pipe Fittings

NPT tapered pipe fittings and mixed-interface adapters manufactured to customer drawings for hydraulic ports, instrumentation, fluid lines and OEM equipment.

NPT is a tapered pipe-thread connection. Pressure connections depend on controlled taper-thread engagement and an application-compatible sealant; the ability to screw threads together does not by itself demonstrate sealing. Straight fittings, elbows, tees, plugs, reducers, hose ends and conversion adapters can be machined to drawing. NPT must not be confused with NPTF, BSPT or straight-thread connections, and every port and conversion end must be confirmed separately.

How NPT Taper Pipe Threads Seal

Both male and female members are tapered. Engagement increases during tightening, and thread flanks plus the helical clearances form part of the connection. Ordinary NPT pressure service normally also needs a compatible sealant. Thread geometry, port condition, materials, sealant and assembly degree work together; sealant cannot correct serious damage, the wrong thread system or the wrong size.

Do not use excessive tightening to correct a mismatch. NPT's wedging action can expand a female port, crack thin walls, damage cast or brittle parts, deform threads and complicate later removal. No universal torque, turns-after-hand-tight or insertion depth applies across sizes, coatings, materials, ports, mating parts and sealants. During maintenance, remove old sealant and inspect threads, ports, burrs, corrosion, cracks, deformation and surface damage before reassembly.

NPT normally uses a 60° form, while BSPT normally uses a 55° Whitworth form. Diameter, pitch, taper definition, governing standard, gauges, sealing requirement and mating component may also differ. See the engineering guide to BSP and NPT thread identification.

NPSM, NPSC, SAE straight thread, ORB, JIC and ORFS straight threads, and BSPP must not be classified as NPT merely because threads are visible.

Thread Sealant and Assembly Considerations

Sealant compatibility

PTFE tape, compatible liquid sealant and paste-type pipe sealant are examples subject to fluid, temperature, material and system compatibility review. Match pressure, fitting and port material, maintenance procedure and customer specification; follow the sealant manufacturer's instructions.

Contamination control

Keep tape fragments and excess liquid out of the flow passage and meet system cleanliness requirements. Some systems require liquid or paste sealant, and some customers prohibit tape.

Controlled tightening

More tightening does not necessarily improve sealing. Thin-wall ports and cast parts require particular review; assemble to the customer or component manufacturer's specification.

Reassembly inspection

Remove old sealant and check thread wear, coating damage, corrosion, deformation and port cracking. Reuse depends on inspection rather than an automatic assumption.

Materials and Surface Finishes

Body materials

Carbon steel and stainless steel are reviewed against the drawing. Brass is considered only after media, mechanical and project review; other materials are subject to drawing and application review. The customer drawing or order specifies the grade.

Finish options

Options may include trivalent zinc plating, zinc-nickel coating, black oxide, phosphating where applicable, natural stainless finish, or passivation when specified and material-compatible.

A finish does not replace correct material selection, thread-sealing design or system corrosion design.

Quality Control for NPT Fittings

Inspection planning may cover drawing and revision review, standard designation and edition, NPT versus NPTF identification, material documentation, external and internal taper threads, appropriate NPT plug or ring gauges, agreed mating references, taper engagement and position, overall and thread length, hex size, bore, flow passage, cross holes, specified wall thickness, edge break, deburring, cleanliness and finish appearance.

Sampling or full dimensional inspection follows the agreed plan. Pressure or leak testing is performed only when explicitly specified; different designs do not automatically receive the same gauges or test plan.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are NPT pipe fittings?

NPT pipe fittings use the American general-purpose tapered pipe-thread system for ports and adapters. Applicable geometry can be manufactured to ASME B1.20.1 when that standard is identified on the customer-approved drawing or order.

How does an NPT connection create a seal?

Male and female tapered threads progressively engage. Thread geometry, flank contact, port condition, materials, compatible sealant, controlled assembly and complete-system validation all affect sealing; engagement alone does not establish sealing performance.

Does an NPT fitting require thread sealant?

An ordinary NPT pressure connection normally uses a compatible pipe-thread sealant. PTFE tape, liquid sealant or paste are only examples subject to fluid, temperature, material and system compatibility review and the sealant manufacturer's instructions.

What is the difference between NPT and NPTF?

Ordinary NPT generally references ASME B1.20.1, while NPTF/Dryseal generally references ASME B1.20.3 and has different crest, root, manufacturing and inspection controls. We review NPTF only when explicitly specified, and do not assume every NPTF application needs no sealant.

Are NPT and BSPT threads interchangeable?

No interchangeability should be assumed. NPT normally has a 60° thread form and BSPT normally has a 55° Whitworth form; diameter, pitch, taper definition, standard, gauges, sealing requirements and mating parts may also differ.

What information is required to quote an NPT fitting?

Provide NPT or NPTF identification, nominal size, male or female form, every port and opposite-end standard, body configuration, material, quantity, application conditions, drawing or verified dimensions, finish and inspection requirements.

Which materials and finishes are available?

Carbon steel and stainless steel are considered from the drawing. Brass requires media, mechanical and project review. Finishes may include trivalent zinc, zinc-nickel, black oxide, applicable phosphating, natural stainless or specified compatible passivation.

Can Wei Xing Machinery manufacture custom NPT adapters?

Yes. We review drawing-specific NPT hydraulic adapters, including NPT-to-BSP, NPT-to-JIC, NPT-to-ORFS, metric and hose interfaces. Each end, sealing location, mating part and assembly requirement must be defined separately.
