Custom ORFS Hydraulic Fittings

O-ring face seal fittings and adapters manufactured to customer drawings for OEM hydraulic hose, tube and equipment connections.

ORFS seals by compressing an O-ring against a flat face; its straight thread supplies assembly clamping force rather than the primary seal. Applicable SAE J1453 or ISO 8434-3 connection geometry can be machined when specified by the drawing or order, including connectors, adapters, elbows, tees, bulkheads and other drawing-defined forms. Pressure capability and interchangeability must be validated for the specific assembly.

Connection Principle

How ORFS O-Ring Face Seal Connections Work

The O-ring sits in a machined groove and is compressed by the mating flat face. The straight thread and nut generate axial clamping force, but the thread itself is not the primary ORFS sealing location. Face damage, groove dimensions, O-ring material and assembly condition can all affect sealing. During service, inspect both the face and O-ring rather than relying only on whether the thread engages. Assembly torque must follow the applicable connection standard, customer assembly specification or system requirement; no universal torque applies to every configuration.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are ORFS hydraulic fittings?

ORFS hydraulic fittings are O-ring face seal connections for joining hydraulic hoses, tubes, equipment ports and adapters. ORFS describes the face-sealing connection, not an independent thread standard.

How does an ORFS fitting create a seal?

A flat mating face compresses an O-ring held in a machined groove. The resulting face seal supports stable sealing when the groove, face, O-ring and assembly conditions are correctly specified.

Are ORFS threads the primary sealing location?

No. The unified straight thread and nut provide axial clamping force; the O-ring and flat face form the primary seal. The face-seal location does not rely on pipe-thread sealant.

Which standards apply to ORFS connections?

Applicable connection geometry can be manufactured according to SAE J1453 and ISO 8434-3 when specified by the customer drawing or order.

What is the difference between ORFS and JIC fittings?

ORFS uses an O-ring compressed at a flat face, while JIC uses mating 37-degree metal flare surfaces. They require different mating geometry and must not be treated as directly interchangeable.

Which information is required to quote a custom ORFS fitting?

Provide a drawing, model, sample with verified dimensions, every port specification, material, finish, O-ring or service conditions, quantity, inspection, documentation, packaging and validation requirements.

How is the O-ring material selected?

Selection is based on fluid compatibility, temperature range, compound, dimensions and the customer or assembly specification. NBR or FKM may be used when specified and confirmed as compatible with the application.

Can Wei Xing Machinery manufacture custom ORFS adapters?

Yes. Wei Xing Machinery can review straight, elbow, tee, bulkhead, swivel and mixed-interface adapters for drawing-based manufacture. Available configurations depend on drawing review and production requirements.
