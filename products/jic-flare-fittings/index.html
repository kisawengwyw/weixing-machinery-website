Custom JIC 37° Flare Fittings

JIC 37-degree flare fittings and mixed-interface adapters manufactured to customer drawings for hydraulic tube, hose and OEM equipment connections.

A JIC connection forms its primary seal at matching 37° metal flare surfaces; unified straight threads mainly generate axial clamping force and are not the primary sealing location. Drawing-defined straight, elbow, tee, swivel, bulkhead and transition configurations can be machined. A 37° JIC end must not be mixed with a 45° flare connection based only on appearance or apparent thread engagement—verify every port and mating component separately.

Connection Principle

How JIC 37° Flare Connections Seal

Metal flare contact

The fitting nose contacts a matching 37° tube flare or compatible hose-end face. The straight thread and nut pull the parts together; sealing does not occur at the straight-thread flanks.

Surface and alignment

Keep the cone and flare clean, undamaged and correctly aligned. Do not force a misaligned line into position by tightening the connection.

Assembly control

Excessive tightening can damage a flare face or deform a tube end, while insufficient force can leave incomplete contact. Requirements depend on size, material, tube construction, mating parts and the applicable assembly specification; this page does not provide a universal torque table.

Service inspection

Before reassembly, inspect the cone, flare, threads, nut, sleeve and tube end for contamination, cracks, deformation or scratches. Serviceability does not mean indefinite reuse.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are JIC 37° flare fittings?

JIC 37° flare fittings are hydraulic connectors whose fitting nose contacts a matching 37° flared tube face or compatible hose-end mating face. Unified straight threads draw the parts together and retain the assembly.

How does a JIC flare fitting create a seal?

The primary seal forms where clean, aligned 37° metal flare surfaces contact under appropriate axial clamping force. Flare angle, surface condition, tube preparation, alignment and assembly force all affect the connection.

Do JIC threads provide the primary seal?

No. The straight threads and nut primarily create axial clamping force; the thread flanks are not the JIC flare connection’s primary sealing location. A tapered NPT or BSPT end on a transition adapter must be assessed separately and may require compatible sealant under the system specification.

Can JIC 37° fittings connect to 45° flare fittings?

No. A 37° JIC connection must not be mixed with a 45° flare connection merely because the parts look similar or threads begin to engage. Verify angle, tube outside diameter, thread size, male or female form, sealing face, mating standard and connector construction.

Which standards apply to JIC 37° connections?

General technical descriptions may reference SAE J514-1 and ISO 8434-2. Production follows the standard designation and edition specified on the customer-approved drawing or order. Legacy drawings may identify SAE J514 and require project review; this page does not claim certification or general conformity.

What information is required to quote a JIC fitting?

Provide a drawing or verified sample data, 37° connection size, tube outside diameter or hose interface, male or female form, every port, opposite-end standard, body configuration, material, finish, quantity, application and inspection requirements.

Which materials and finishes are available?

Carbon steel and stainless steel can be reviewed, with other materials subject to the drawing, media and application. Finishes may include trivalent zinc, zinc-nickel, black oxide, applicable phosphating, natural stainless or specified material-compatible passivation.

Can Wei Xing Machinery manufacture custom JIC adapters?

Yes. We review drawing-specific JIC hydraulic adapters, including JIC-to-ORFS, JIC-to-NPT, JIC-to-BSP and JIC-to-metric designs. Each end, sealing location, mating component and validation requirement must be identified separately.
